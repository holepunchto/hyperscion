So I switched to `--new`, which printed `watching main after #118 (new lands only)` and timed out after 300 s. The pm's CR-1 (#115) had landed between my last sync (#100) and #118, so `--new` skipped right past it. I only found the change request by running `hs sync` afterwards. That cost me about 5 minutes of reacting late.
- **Most wanted:** a wait that starts from my synced base, ignores my own changesets and anything already in my files, and returns on the first teammate or pm change after that base (e.g. `hs watch --path X --next`). Better still, make that the default.
