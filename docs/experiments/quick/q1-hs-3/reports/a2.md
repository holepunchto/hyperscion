The CONTRACTS.md/NOTES.md edits that overlapped with a1's #10 merged on their own ("merged teammates' changes into your edits: /CONTRACTS.md (#10 a1)"). `hs watch --path /CHANGE-REQUEST.md --count 1` (and later `--path /src/pricing.js`) was a clean way to wait without polling.
