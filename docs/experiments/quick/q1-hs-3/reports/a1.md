# a1 report

- **Best:** `hs watch --path /src/scheduler.js --path /CHANGE-REQUEST.md --count 1 --timeout 300` was an ideal "block until the thing I depend on lands" primitive, and `hs land --check "npm test"` auto-merged a3's concurrent CONTRACTS.md/NOTES.md edits, ran tests on the merged tree and ended with `#3 final`. I never had to think about merging.
- **Worst friction:** the hub stalled mid-run. `hs land ... --check "npm test"` printed `landed #10 / pushed: a holder has the changes / #10 provisional: waiting for hub to ack (it settles on its own)`, and `hs status` printed `!! hub not settling: lands after #7 have been provisional for 86s (hub down or behind)`. Both were clear and nothing broke (it later showed `last land: #10 final`). Still, for a while I couldn't tell whether my land was safe to build on. Coordination friction on CR-1: a2 (stops[].flightMinutes) and a3 (via my geo.flightMinutesToStops) both computed flight minutes in parallel, so some work was duplicated. hs doesn't help you see in-flight intent before a land.
- **Most wanted:** a lightweight "I'm working on X" claim/intent signal visible in the feed, so a CR doesn't get implemented twice. Also an ETA or reason for a hub stall in `hs status`.
