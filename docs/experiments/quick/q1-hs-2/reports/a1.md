- **Best:** `hs watch --path /src/scheduler.js --path /CHANGE-REQUEST.md --count 1 --timeout 300` and then `hs sync` made waiting on teammates very easy: no polling, and the output named exactly what arrived (`#4 a2 /CONTRACTS.md ... :: scheduler: plan(day) greedy ...`). `land --check "npm test"` merged a3's concurrent CONTRACTS.md/NOTES.md edits into my first land with no fuss (`merged teammates' changes into your edits: /CONTRACTS.md (#2 a3), /NOTES.md (#2 a3)` ... `#3 final`).
- **Worst friction:** during the hub stall, the different views of settlement disagreed. `hs watch --for-me --count 1` printed `@ #7 final`, and the feed had `{"type":"settled","seq":9}` and `{"type":"hub","state":"settling","settled":9}`. Yet the very next `hs status` still said `last land: #7 provisional, waiting for hub to ack` / `!! hub not settling: lands after #4 have been provisional for 123s`, and `hs log -n 3 --oneline` tagged #7-#9 `[provisional]`. Only after another `hs sync` did it show `last land: #7 final`. Before that, `land` had ended with `#7 provisional: waiting for hub to ack (it settles on its own)`, which was clear and harmless. The hub outage itself cost me nothing.
- **Most wanted:** let `status` and `log` use the settlement state the local feed already has (or refresh it cheaply without a full sync), so every view gives the same answer to "is my land safe?".
