- **Best:** `hs land --check "npm test"` did the merge plus test for me and ended with a clear `#281 final` / `#442 final`. `hs watch --path /src/pricing.js --author a3 --count 1` was a clean way to block until a3's CR-1 pricing landed, and NOTES.md union-merge never conflicted.
- **Worst friction:** with the ~20-contributor swarm, every `land --check` chased a moving main: `check passed in 0.4s` / `main moved to #144 while checking; checking again` repeated 4 times, then `!! main moved to #279 during every check; landing on the last checked state`. Each land took about 3-4 min of wall time and ran past my 120 s tool timeout, even though the only changes were unrelated `/swarm/` files. It also held the worktree lock the whole time (`hs: another hs process is using this worktree`), so I couldn't even `hs cat` while it ran.
